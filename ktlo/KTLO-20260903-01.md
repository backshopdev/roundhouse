## Status

## Context

During WP-06 review, finding N4 identified that the `test-sync` script was not
documented in AGENTS.md. The initial policy treated every documentation gap as
a blocker. The review policy has since been refined to distinguish material
accuracy/completeness gaps from clarity or style suggestions that do not
materially mislead consumers.

## Policy

Use `Blocker` for material documentation accuracy or completeness gaps,
including material mismatches between implementation and relevant
documentation (implementation maps, architecture documents, or specifications
as applicable), or omissions that leave consumers with a materially incorrect
or incomplete understanding.

Clarity or style suggestions that do not materially mislead consumers are
non-blocking and use one existing severity (`Critical | Major | Medium | Low |
Nit`) according to impact. Each finding has exactly one classification:
`Blocker` or one existing severity. A `Blocker` prevents approval; no separate
status and severity are assigned to the same finding.

## Resolution

Updated the peer-review criteria and mandatory review-findings format to apply
this classification consistently.

## Rationale

Documentation is part of the deliverable when its accuracy or completeness is
material to consumer understanding. A material mismatch can cause incorrect
use and must block approval; non-misleading presentation improvements should
remain actionable without being promoted to blockers.
