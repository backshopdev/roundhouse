# KTLO-20261018-01: Socratic interview skill: encode the free-form interview style demonstrated in the 2026-10-17/18 taxonomy interview

Last modified: 2026-10-18
Status: Active

## Description

The Phase 4 `socratic-interview` skill (the DEC-20261017-04 commitment to
extract the Socratic-interview behavior into a new Core skill named
`socratic-interview` during Phase 4) must encode a specific interview style,
demonstrated and endorsed by the human during the 2026-10-17/18
package-taxonomy interview. The style
properties below are the normative content of this item:

1. **Free-form conversational Q&A.** The interviewer asks open-ended questions
   in prose and the human answers in free text.
2. **No OpenCode question-tool option menus for elicitation.** The `question`
   tool's multiple-choice prompts constrain answers and must not be used for
   interview questions; reserve it for discrete gate decisions
   (approve / request changes) where options are genuinely exhaustive.
3. **One question at a time** — never a volley.
4. **Reflective playback between turns.** Before each new question, the
   interviewer restates what it heard, names emerging principles and patterns,
   and plays back its understanding for correction — the playback is where
   shared understanding gets built.
5. **The interviewer contributes, not just extracts.** It offers observations,
   proposed principles, honest opinions — and withdraws them when the human's
   principle reveals a better reading (this happened: the interviewer's
   `software-media-delivery` rename proposal was withdrawn in favor of
   dissolution under the taxonomy's own nature-based principle).
6. **Concrete-instance probes.** Abstract design questions get grounded in
   real usage scenarios ("walk me through a real installation end-to-end") —
   the concrete instance exposes rules the abstract discussion misses.
7. **Let the human's principles do the work.** When the human states a
   principle (e.g., "the prefix should indicate what group they're in"; "org
   is a suffix on skills, a prefix on packages"), apply it systematically to
   everything under discussion rather than re-asking case by case.

## Trigger

The human's explicit mid-interview request (2026-10-18): "I want to ensure
that the style of interview we've been doing here is encapsulated in the
socratic interview skill we discussed. This interview is pretty much EXACTLY
what I want (free-form question/answer, not using the OpenCode prompts that
limit answers)."

## Evidence

The 2026-10-17/18 package-taxonomy interview in the orchestrator session:
~15 exchanges evolving the harness taxonomy from four `domain-*` packages to
the nature-based composable taxonomy (core, lang-, framework-, cms-, tool-,
software-, docs-, format-, protocol-, backshopdev-, domain- redefined as
business domains), with every one of the 48 current skills receiving a ruled
fate. The interview demonstrated all seven style properties listed in the
Description; the human endorsed it as the exemplar ("pretty much EXACTLY what
I want").

## Why this matters now

The skill does not exist yet (Phase 4). Without this capture, the style lives
only in session memory and the Phase 4 author would reconstruct it from the
Phase 4 commitment in DEC-20261017-04, losing
the properties the human explicitly endorsed. Capturing now, while the
exemplar is fresh, preserves the requirement.

## Resolution criteria

The Phase 4 skill exists as the Core `socratic-interview` skill named in
DEC-20261017-04, and its text encodes all seven style properties; a review
of the skill against this item's list passes. The item closes when that
review is recorded.
