# Decision Quality Review Lens

## Lens stance

Evaluate whether a decision record captures enough context, reasoning, and
consequence to serve as a useful historical artifact. A good decision record
lets a future reader understand the decision, evaluate whether it still holds,
and determine when to reconsider.

## Review criteria

### Context

- Is the triggering situation described clearly?
- Would a reader unfamiliar with the project understand why this decision was
  needed?
- Is relevant background included without unnecessary detail?

### Decision drivers

- Are the forces that shaped the decision explicit?
- Are drivers specific enough to evaluate (not vague goals)?
- Can a reader determine whether a driver has changed?

### Alternatives

- Are at least two alternatives described plus the chosen option?
- Does each alternative include advantages and disadvantages?
- Is "do nothing" considered when appropriate?
- Are the alternatives genuinely different approaches, not straw men?

### Tradeoffs

- Does the rationale connect the chosen option to the drivers?
- Are trade-offs explicit, not hidden?
- Would a reasonable person disagree with the choice? If so, is the
  disagreement acknowledged?

### Consequences

- Are positive and negative consequences both described?
- Are follow-up actions identified?
- Are risks acknowledged?

### Assumptions

- Are assumptions explicit?
- Can each assumption be tested or observed?
- What happens if an assumption proves false?

### Reversibility

- Is the decision reversible or irreversible?
- If irreversible, is the irreversibility acknowledged?
- What is the cost of reversing the decision?

### Reconsideration triggers

- Are triggers concrete and observable?
- Would a reader know when to revisit this decision?
- Are triggers tied to drivers and assumptions?

### Historical usefulness

- Would this record be useful to a reader six months or two years from now?
- Does it capture reasoning that is not obvious from the code?
- Does it prevent the same discussion from happening again?
