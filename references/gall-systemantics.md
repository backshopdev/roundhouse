- **Author**: John Gall
- **Reference Type**: book (systems theory / systems engineering)
- **Title**: *Systemantics: How Systems Work and Especially How They Fail*
- **Link**: <https://archive.org/details/systemanticshows00gall>

- **Secondary summary**: [Novel Investor notes](https://novelinvestor.com/notes/systemantics-how-systems-work-and-especially-how-they-fail-by-john-gall), published 2022-10-12.

- **Informs principles**: GP01 (qualified conceptual lens; not empirical evidence)

## Source characterization

The catalog record supplies edition and publication metadata. Wikipedia characterizes Gall's work as experience- and anecdote-based. Novel Investor's notes describe the book as humorous and summarize ideas about system failure, unintended consequences, and attending to outcomes and the processes that produce them. The book's full text was not accessed, so these secondary descriptions do not verify its claims or their context. Treat the reported systems ideas as a conceptual lens, not empirical proof.

## Project interpretation

As a design prompt for GP01, the summary's warnings about systems creating new problems suggest adding workflow complexity only for specific, evidenced problems or needs surfaced through use or identified during design/evaluation. This need not wait for post-release failure and does not justify speculative process. This is a project interpretation, not a principle demonstrated by the inaccessible primary text.

Evaluation prompt: “Do our feedback signals reveal actual outcomes, or only what the workflow reports?”
